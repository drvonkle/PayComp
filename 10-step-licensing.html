Your 10-step roadmap to a P&C license

How a payroll company becomes a licensed CompConnect partner in its home state. You complete the coursework and pay your state's fees. PayComp guides you through every step.

Prepare
1

Choose who gets licensed

An owner or long-term team member. Consider licensing a second person for continuity.

2

Confirm your state's requirements

Check your Department of Insurance and NIPR for lines of authority, fees and conditions.

Qualify
3

Complete education and fingerprints

Where your state requires them. Pre-licensing hours and processing times vary.

4

Pass the licensing exam

Confirm which exam and lines of authority apply. Some states offer exemptions.

Apply
5

Apply for the individual license

Through NIPR or your state's process. Wait for approval before acting as a producer.

6

Apply for the agency license

Where required, license the business and name a designated responsible licensed producer (DRLP).

Set Up
7

Arrange E&O coverage

Put the right errors and omissions coverage in place before insurance work begins.

8

Complete partner agreements

PayComp coordinates the sub-producer agreement and any required carrier or state appointments.

Launch
9

Launch after compliance clearance

Confirm active licenses, appointments, agreements and compensation terms before selling.

10

Stay licensed and compliant

Track renewals, continuing education, DRLP changes and any nonresident licensing needs.
