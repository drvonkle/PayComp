Opening the Conversation

What Does PayComp CompConnect Do?

“You already own the payroll relationship. CompConnect helps you extend that relationship into workers’ compensation without building an insurance operation yourself. You refer the opportunity, and our team handles the insurance process and ongoing service.”

Benefits to the Payroll Provider

  • Add value to existing clients. Offer a workers’ compensation solution where payroll already lives, without asking clients to switch payroll providers.
  • Simple referral model. Introduce the client; CompConnect handles quoting, placement, onboarding coordination, renewals, audits, claims support, and carrier relationships.
  • 160+ carrier network. Access regional and national workers’ compensation markets through PayComp’s team.
  • Dedicated partner support. A CompConnect representative manages referrals from quote to bind.
  • Revenue opportunity. Referral compensation is available for qualifying partner programs; eligibility and structure must be confirmed.
  • No independent agency buildout required for referrals. PayComp holds the licenses for its program; partner activities and compensation remain subject to applicable requirements.

Benefits to Their Clients

  • Payroll-connected pay-as-you-go. WOPR uses actual payroll activity to calculate workers’ compensation premiums each run.
  • Less administrative work. Automated reporting reduces repetitive self-reporting and manual follow-up.
  • Cash-flow advantage. The brochure illustrates a $350 integration fee versus traditional $5,000–$25,000+ down payments; confirm applicability for each case.
  • Cleaner audits and greater visibility. Consistent reporting helps reduce year-end surprises; audits are not eliminated.
  • ATLAS dashboard. View premium activity, payment status, coverage status, and reports with role-based access.

How the Partnership Works

  1. You refer: Identify a payroll client with a workers’ compensation or other P&C insurance need.
  2. We quote and bind: CompConnect shops available markets and coordinates coverage placement.
  3. We connect payroll: The reporting workflow aligns with payroll activity.
  4. We manage: PayComp supports ongoing service, reporting, renewals, audits, and carrier coordination.

Discovery Questions for IPPA

  • How are you helping payroll clients who ask for workers’ compensation today?
  • Are your clients still manually self-reporting payroll to carriers?
  • Would a referral program that your team does not have to service be valuable?
  • How important are client retention and additional partner revenue opportunities to your growth strategy?

Common Questions / Quick Answers

  • Do we need to become an insurance agency? Not to participate in the brochure’s referral model; discuss permitted activities and compensation eligibility with PayComp.
  • Does our client change payroll providers? No. The goal is to preserve the existing payroll relationship.
  • Does this replace the carrier or policy? No. The reporting workflow supports coverage; the issuing carrier controls underwriting and policy terms.
  • Does it eliminate audits? No. More consistent reporting can help reduce surprises and improve audit readiness.
