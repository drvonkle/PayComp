IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

Complete Payroll

Attending: Austin Fish · Rich White

Company Snapshot

  • Privately held, family-managed payroll and HCM provider founded in 1992, headquartered in Perry, New York.
  • Complete Payroll supports isolved, Evolution (Asure), and other HR/timekeeping tools.
  • The company actively works with insurance agents, benefits consultants, CPAs, HR consultants and other referral partners.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • Complete Payroll already has workers’ comp solutions, including isolved SmartPay and NYSIF-approved pay-as-you-go.
  • Explore broader placement options when existing channels cannot place an account.
  • Discuss payroll-to-carrier reporting and reconciliation friction across isolved and Evolution customers.
  • Ask whether geographic expansion has exposed new workers’ comp availability or service gaps.

Watch-Outs

  • Do not pitch pay-as-you-go as a new concept.
  • Existing SmartPay, NYSIF and broker/vendor relationships may already meet most needs.
  • Clarify who controls placement, incumbent-agent protections, data sharing and licensing before discussing compensation.

Questions to Ask

  • Which workers’ comp needs are already fully covered by SmartPay and your other vendor relationships?
  • When a client cannot be placed through your current channels, what happens next?
  • Do you see differences in insurance workflows for isolved versus Evolution customers?
  • Would a broker-supported overflow or referral pilot be worth evaluating without disrupting current agents?

Suggested Opening

Austin, I saw that Complete Payroll has already built out pay-as-you-go workers’ comp with SmartPay and other partners, so we’re not here to replace what’s working. With Comp Connect, we help payroll providers connect employers to workers’ comp options and support payroll-linked reporting.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Document existing comp vendors/carriers, uncovered states or industries, isolved/Evolution workflow, current broker relationship, desired pilot and agreed follow-up date.
