IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

ConnectPay

Attending: Michael Young · Paul Altavena

Company Snapshot

  • Founded in 2008 and headquartered in Mansfield, Massachusetts.
  • ConnectPay states 12,000+ client businesses and operates multiple regional offices with nationwide payroll service.
  • Core platform is RELAY powered by ConnectPay, with online payroll, employee self-service, reports, CPA access and multi-state payroll.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • ConnectPay already offers workers’ compensation pay-as-you-go and works with existing brokers or broker matches.
  • Explore additional carrier options for difficult classes, state-specific accounts or renewals.
  • Ask how declined accounts, coverage gaps and exceptions are handled.
  • A scalable escalation/referral pathway may be valuable given the size of its client base.

Watch-Outs

  • Existing pay-as-you-go functionality and broker relationships may limit the opportunity.
  • Confirm partner exclusivity, carrier access, RELAY data exchange, customer ownership and licensing/commission rules.
  • Do not promise integrations, specific carrier access, compensation or exclusivity before validation.

Questions to Ask

  • How is your existing pay-as-you-go workers’ comp program structured today?
  • Where do you see the most coverage or carrier-placement challenges?
  • Do you have an exclusive insurance partner or multiple broker/carrier relationships?
  • How does RELAY exchange payroll and premium information with insurance partners?
  • If we could solve a defined gap, who should join a technical and partnership discovery call?

Suggested Opening

Michael, I noticed ConnectPay already connects payroll, workers’ comp and trusted brokers. We’re not looking to replace that. I’m curious where your current model runs into exceptions—whether that’s carrier appetite, specific industries or accounts that are difficult to place.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Identify one concrete unmet use case, learn the current PAYG provider and insurance arrangement, confirm decision-makers and agree to a post-event discovery conversation.
