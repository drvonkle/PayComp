IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

Commonwealth Payroll & HR

Attending: Jeff Plakans

Company Snapshot

  • Privately operated payroll and HR services provider focused on small and midsize employers.
  • Jeff Plakans founded the business in 2006; its site highlights its 20th anniversary in 2026.
  • The company has a documented relationship with isolved and offers payroll, HR and benefits-related tools.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • PEO exits may create a need for standalone workers’ comp policies and smooth payroll-based billing.
  • Comp Connect could potentially coordinate workers’ comp options while Commonwealth keeps the payroll relationship.
  • Broader placement workflows may help if current partners cannot accommodate certain classes, states or employer sizes.

Watch-Outs

  • Public materials did not establish the exact workers’ comp carrier roster or current pay-as-you-go integration.
  • Respect existing brokers and carrier arrangements.
  • Insurance compensation depends on applicable state licensing rules; do not promise commission sharing before review.

Questions to Ask

  • When clients leave a PEO, who helps them arrange their standalone workers’ comp policy?
  • Are you already offering pay-as-you-go workers’ comp through isolved or another partner?
  • Which industries or states create the biggest placement or billing challenges?
  • Would a supplemental carrier-access and broker-support option help your team?

Suggested Opening

Jeff, we saw your conversation about helping employers move away from PEOs, and that really stood out. One challenge in that transition can be getting workers’ comp in place without adding friction to payroll. Comp Connect helps payroll providers coordinate workers’ comp solutions and pay-as-you-go workflows.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Capture Jeff’s interest level, current insurance partner(s), existing pay-as-you-go solution, state/industry pain points, integration contact, requested materials and next step.
