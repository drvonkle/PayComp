IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

Payentry

Attending: Geoffrey Duke · Renita Wojtuszewski · Brett Binder

Company Snapshot

  • Privately held payroll, HR, benefits, retirement, insurance and workforce-management provider; MPAY/Payentry founded in 1994.
  • Headquartered in Peachtree Corners, Georgia.
  • Payentry offers its own SaaS payroll platform, including licensing to other payroll providers.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • Payentry already offers payroll-linked workers’ comp and has its own licensed insurance team.
  • Explore supplemental markets for classes or locations the current panel cannot accommodate.
  • Overflow and hard-to-place accounts may create a useful referral path.
  • Evaluate whether Payentry SaaS licensees need independent workers’ comp placement and servicing support.

Watch-Outs

  • Payentry is not an insurance novice; generic pay-as-you-go messaging risks sounding uninformed.
  • Confirm existing carrier/broker exclusivity, program ownership, data permissions and approved compensation structure.
  • Do not imply a PayComp integration is already certified.

Questions to Ask

  • Where do your current workers’ comp markets leave gaps by state, class code or employer size?
  • Do your SaaS licensees have access to the same insurance program, or do they source coverage independently?
  • When an account is declined or difficult to place, what is the current escalation path?
  • Would it be useful to evaluate a limited referral pilot for accounts your current program cannot serve?

Suggested Opening

Geoffrey, Renita, we saw that Payentry already has a strong payroll-integrated workers’ comp offering, so we’re not here to duplicate what you’ve built. I’d love to understand where your current program has limitations—specific industries, states or accounts that are tough to place.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Capture current insurance program, gaps by state/industry, licensee needs, integration/reporting approach, decision owner and any agreed pilot or demo.
