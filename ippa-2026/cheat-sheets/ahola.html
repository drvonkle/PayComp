IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

Ahola Corporation

Attending: Jeff Ahola · Michael Paull

Company Snapshot

  • Third-generation, family-owned payroll and HR provider founded in 1967 and headquartered in Brecksville, Ohio.
  • Services include payroll, tax filing, time and attendance, HR, benefits administration, onboarding and HR consulting.
  • Platform: isolved People Cloud.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • Explore whether payroll-driven premium reporting could reduce manual effort for clients.
  • A workers’ comp referral pathway could help Ahola support clients without acting as the broker.
  • Potential revenue should only be discussed subject to licensing, carrier rules and legal review.
  • Confirm whether Comp Connect supports Ahola’s specific isolved setup; no integration is assumed.

Watch-Outs

  • Current workers’ comp broker/carrier relationships are not confirmed.
  • Licensing and referral eligibility are not confirmed.
  • Do not assume a verified Comp Connect–isolved integration.

Questions to Ask

  • When a payroll client asks for workers’ comp or pay-as-you-go billing, what happens today?
  • Do you work with a broker or carrier partner, and where is there friction?
  • How important is fitting into your existing isolved workflow?
  • Who owns strategic partnerships and insurance integrations?

Suggested Opening

Jeff, I know Ahola has built its reputation around personal service and making payroll and HR easier for growing businesses. We work with payroll providers to help connect their clients with workers’ comp solutions and simplify pay-as-you-go workflows.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Secure a follow-up discovery call with Jeff and the partnership/product owner; clarify insurance workflows, platform needs, client segments and licensing constraints before discussing economics.
