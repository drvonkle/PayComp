IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

AllianceHCM

Attending: Matt Umholtz · Vickie Sorokin

Company Snapshot

  • Headquartered in The Woodlands, Texas.
  • AllianceHCM is a payroll and HCM platform serving 5,000+ customers with payroll, HR, onboarding, time, benefits and reporting.
  • Focuses on restaurants, franchises and frontline employers.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • AllianceHCM already markets insurance-agency partnerships and workers’ comp integrations.
  • Explore complementary broker or carrier connectivity where existing relationships do not meet employer needs.
  • Discuss payroll-to-workers-comp reporting and pay-as-you-go workflows, subject to integration feasibility.
  • Restaurant, hospitality and franchise clients with variable payroll may be strong discovery targets.

Watch-Outs

  • Existing carrier and broker integrations may overlap with Comp Connect.
  • Confirm technical access, data permissions, state-by-state licensing, referral compensation rules and servicing ownership.
  • A PayComp connection is not confirmed and needs technical and commercial validation.

Questions to Ask

  • How are your clients handling workers’ comp today, especially pay-as-you-go reporting and carrier connectivity?
  • Are there states, carriers or franchise client segments where your existing partners cannot meet demand?
  • When evaluating a new integration or referral partner, who owns technical approval, insurance compliance and commercial terms?

Suggested Opening

You have built a strong payroll and HR platform and already support insurance partners. We are curious where workers’ comp connectivity is working well for your clients, and where gaps remain, especially for multi-location restaurant and franchise operators.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Ask whether broader workers’ comp partner coverage aligns with product strategy, identify recurring client objections and propose a 30-minute discovery session—not an assumed integration agreement.
