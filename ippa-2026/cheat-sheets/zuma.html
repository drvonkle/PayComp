IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

Zuma Payroll & Processing

Attending: Chris Caputo · Scott Evans

Company Snapshot

  • Founded in 2006 and headquartered in Melville, New York.
  • Provides payroll, HR, onboarding, time and labor, benefits administration, and construction/union payroll.
  • Zuma appeared on the 2026 Inc. 5000 and supports construction-specific payroll workflows.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • Zuma already offers pay-as-you-go workers’ compensation and works with clients’ existing brokers.
  • Placement support may help construction, union or hard-to-place risks.
  • Comp Connect should complement broker relationships rather than replace them.
  • Explore multi-state, carrier appetite, class-code and reporting workflow gaps.

Watch-Outs

  • Do not pitch workers’ comp as a missing service.
  • Confirm existing broker/carrier coverage, actual service gaps, technical feasibility and referral compensation eligibility.
  • Broker-replacement or exclusive positioning is a poor fit.

Questions to Ask

  • Which workers’ comp classes or states are hardest for your broker partners to place?
  • What happens when a construction or union client cannot secure competitive coverage?
  • Are there pay-go reporting or carrier workflows you wish were easier?
  • Would a small pilot with a clear referral handoff be worth exploring?

Suggested Opening

Chris, we saw that Zuma already does pay-as-you-go workers’ comp and has built a broker-friendly approach, especially for complex construction payroll. We’re not here to replace what’s working. Comp Connect may be useful when a broker needs another option for a tough class or additional carrier relationship.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Confirm Zuma’s broker/carrier model, priority coverage gaps, technology owner and decision process; ask whether Chris would welcome a follow-up demo or pilot.
