IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

CheckmateHCM

Attending: Joshua Robinson

Company Snapshot

  • Joshua Robinson is Owner & President. Checkmate was founded in 1994 and is headquartered in Concord, New Hampshire.
  • Positioning combines dedicated account-manager service with nationwide payroll processing, tax filing, HRIS, time and attendance, benefits administration, ACA reporting and workforce scheduling.
  • Checkmate identifies itself as a UKG Premier Partner.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • Checkmate already advertises pay-as-you-go workers’ compensation. Position Comp Connect as supplemental, not replacement.
  • Test for broader placement options when existing partners cannot fit an employer.
  • Complex payroll exposures such as class codes, job costing, multi-state and harder-to-place risks may create openings.

Watch-Outs

  • Existing workers’ comp relationships may limit the opportunity.
  • Ask who owns insurance servicing and underwriting decisions.
  • Do not promise integration, placement or revenue until licensing, state law, carrier contracts and technical feasibility are confirmed.

Questions to Ask

  • How is your pay-as-you-go workers’ comp program structured today, and where do clients still run into coverage challenges?
  • Are there particular industries, states or account sizes that your current insurance partners struggle to place?
  • When clients need a different carrier or more complex underwriting, what does your team do now?
  • Would a supplemental broker/carrier resource be useful if it preserves your client relationship?

Suggested Opening

Josh, we saw that Checkmate already makes workers’ comp part of its broader payroll and HR offering, so we’re not here to replace something that’s working. What we’d love to understand is where your current program gets stretched—whether that’s tougher classes, carrier options, or unusual payroll situations.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Offer a focused discovery call around one underserved account type, then validate carrier fit, technical feasibility and compliant partnership terms before proposing a pilot.
