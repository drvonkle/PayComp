IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

Aprio

Attending: Valerie Saunders, CPP · Sheila Cox

Company Snapshot

  • National accounting, tax, advisory, outsourced accounting, HR/payroll and technology consulting firm. CEO: Richard Kopelman; headquarters: Atlanta, Georgia.
  • Charlesbank reported 2,300+ employees, 250+ partners and offices in 21 cities as of August 2025.
  • Aprio markets payroll processing, payroll tax compliance, HR administration, benefits integration and workforce planning.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • Workers’ comp adjacency: Aprio already advises employers on payroll and HR. A qualified workers’ compensation referral workflow could fill a complementary client need.
  • Construction, manufacturing and hospitality clients may value accurate payroll-based premium reporting and coverage reviews.
  • Explore a compliant referral structure; do not assume insurance commission sharing is permitted or approved.

Watch-Outs

  • Confirm whether Aprio already refers workers’ comp/P&C business, through whom, and whether exclusivity or insurance licensing restrictions apply.
  • Clarify which client segments use which payroll systems.
  • Do not promise a native PayComp integration without technical validation.

Questions to Ask

  • When your payroll clients need workers’ comp coverage or a better pay-as-you-go process, how do you handle that today?
  • Which client industries or payroll systems create the most friction between payroll reporting and insurance?
  • Would a co-managed referral process be valuable if your team kept the client relationship and we handled insurance follow-through?

Suggested Opening

You already help employers make payroll, HR and financial systems work better together. One area where clients can still run into friction is workers’ compensation: finding the right coverage, managing premiums and keeping payroll reporting aligned. Comp Connect is designed to help bridge that gap. We’d love to learn how your team handles workers’ comp referrals today and see whether there’s a way to support your clients without disrupting your existing relationships.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Identify the payroll/HR sponsor, current workers’ comp process, pain point, target client segment, relevant software and agreed next action.
