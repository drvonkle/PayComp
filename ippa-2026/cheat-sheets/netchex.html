IPPA 2026 · Comp Connect

Netchex

Attending: Joseph King · Andrea Freese

Company Snapshot

  • Privately held payroll and HCM platform offering payroll, HR, time, hiring, benefits and compliance.
  • Founded in 2003 and headquartered in Covington, Louisiana.
  • Netchex reports more than 7,500 client organizations and serves frontline and multi-location employers.

Where Comp Connect May Fit

  • Netchex already has a verified NEXT Insurance integration for workers’ comp and pay-as-you-go.
  • Explore supplemental placement for risks outside the existing channel.
  • Broker-friendly referrals may help clients who want to preserve an existing broker relationship.
  • Multi-location restaurant and hospitality payroll may create reporting and PAYGO workflow opportunities.

Watch-Outs

  • Do not pitch basic PAYGO as a missing feature.
  • Ask whether NEXT is exclusive, what states and industries are covered, and what happens with declined accounts.
  • Technical compatibility and referral economics require validation.

Questions to Ask

  • How does your NEXT Insurance relationship work when a client falls outside its underwriting appetite?
  • Is that arrangement exclusive, or do you already offer multiple workers’ comp placement paths?
  • For multi-location restaurant and hospitality customers, where do you see the biggest insurance service gaps?
  • If we could help with declined or complex accounts, what would a successful pilot look like?

Suggested Opening

Netchex has already done the hard work of connecting payroll and workers’ comp, including your NEXT partnership. We’re not here to replace what’s working. We’d love to understand what happens when a client needs another market, has a more complicated risk, or wants to preserve an existing broker relationship.

Meeting Goal / Handoff

Capture the insurance decision-maker, NEXT exclusivity/coverage gaps, target industries and states, workflow, integration owner and a specific promised next action.
