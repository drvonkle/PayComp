Data use principles

Confidence starts with clear boundaries before integrations ever move data.

PayComp's technology layer connects brokers, carriers, payroll providers, and clients. That only works when every party knows how data is used, where it moves, and why it is needed.

Purpose-limited use

Payroll, policy, broker, carrier, and client data is used to operate PayComp workflows, not to create unrelated products or advertising profiles.

Integration boundaries

Data movement is tied to specific business handoffs: intake, payroll sync, premium calculation, remittance, reporting, and support.

Access discipline

Operational access is treated as controlled and accountable, supporting a clear separation between platform service delivery and unauthorized use.

Data Security & Compliance

PayComp is building its platform to be certified and compliant with globally recognized security, audit, and privacy standards. The goal is simple: earn trust before data ever moves.
