DATA USE PRINCIPLES

Clear boundaries before integrations move data.

Trust depends on knowing why data is needed, how it moves, and who is permitted to access it.

Purpose-limited use

Payroll, policy, broker, carrier, and client information is used to operate PayComp workflows rather than unrelated advertising or resale activity.

Integration boundaries

Data movement is tied to defined business handoffs such as intake, payroll sync, premium calculation, remittance, reporting, and support.

Access discipline

Operational access is treated as controlled and accountable, with separation between authorized service delivery and unauthorized use.
