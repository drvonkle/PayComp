The PayComp Process for Agencies

Six operational checkpoints from sold policy to active payroll billing, without workflow chaos.

1

Agent Sells the Policy with PayComp

Client proposal from the agent includes PayComp integration information.

2

Agent Completes PayComp Client Intake Form

The intake form captures client and payroll provider details so integration starts quickly.

3

Broker or Agency Submits Intake Form

Your agency binds workers' compensation with Pay-As-You-Go. PayComp supports all carrier binding instructions.

4

PayComp Onboards the Client

Client and payroll provider data are validated to ensure a smooth implementation flow.

5

Client Receives Welcome Email

PayComp communicates directly with your client and coordinates setup with their payroll provider.

6

Coverage Goes Live

Payroll-based billing begins automatically with accurate premium calculations and fewer audit surprises.
