What this means for your business

A cleaner operating layer between payroll data, carrier rules, and broker service.

Actual wages, every payroll run

With every payroll run, WOPR calculates your exact workers' comp premium from actual wages, not estimates.

Payroll integration without re-entry

WOPR integrates with payroll providers to automatically pull verified wage data each run, no manual entry, spreadsheets, or delays.

Classification-aware calculations

WOPR applies the correct workers' comp codes to each employee based on role and wages, so premiums reflect true risk.

Carrier-ready reporting

PayComp structures wage and premium data for the carrier path that fits best: manual, third-party, API, branded portal, or custom build.
