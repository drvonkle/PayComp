The PayComp process for Wholesalers/MGAs

Six checkpoints from policy sale to live payroll-based billing.

A structured path that keeps the agency, client, carrier, and payroll provider aligned without adding a custom workflow for every case.

1

Agent sells the policy with PayComp

The client proposal includes PayComp integration information so Pay-As-You-Go is positioned from the first conversation.

2

Agent completes PayComp client intake

The intake captures both client and payroll provider details so PayComp can integrate quickly and cleanly.

3

Broker or agency binds policy with the carrier

The agency binds the workers' compensation policy using Pay-As-You-Go, while PayComp supports binding instructions for carrier partners.

4

Broker or agency submits intake to PayComp

Client and payroll provider information is sent to PayComp for validation, setup, and integration kickoff.

5

Client receives a PayComp welcome email

PayComp communicates directly with each client so setup and payroll provider connection stay clear.

6

Coverage is live

Payroll-based billing begins automatically, helping clients avoid large upfront premiums and audit surprises.
