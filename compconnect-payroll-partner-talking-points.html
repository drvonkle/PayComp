The One-Line Pitch CompConnect gives payroll providers a turnkey workers' comp program to offer their clients, backed by 160+ regional and national carriers. You refer. We handle everything else.

Benefits to the Payroll Partner

  • A new revenue stream. Referral compensation is available for qualifying partner programs, paid monthly on client policies.
  • Zero licensing burden. PayComp holds the licenses. Your team simply refers.
  • Nothing to build or staff. PayComp quotes, binds, and handles renewals, audits, claims support, and carrier relationships.
  • A dedicated account executive on every referral. One PayComp rep manages each referral from quote to bind.
  • Easy referrals. Clients are sent through PayComp's dedicated referral link—one intake path instead of scattered emails and rework.
  • Payroll runs as usual. Your clients stay on your platform. WOPR connects to the payroll data; nothing changes in how you process payroll.
  • Stickier clients. Adding workers' comp embedded in payroll makes your offering more complete and harder to leave.
  • Any industry, any size. Coverage options from small businesses to large employers nationwide.
  • More than workers' comp. Partners can also refer general liability, property, professional liability, and other P&C needs.
  • Visibility through ATLAS. Role-based dashboard showing premiums, ACH payment activity, coverage status, and reports.

Benefits to the Partner's Clients

  • Lower upfront cost. A $350 integration fee instead of a $5,000–$25,000+ down payment.
  • Premiums match actual payroll. Calculated on real wages each payroll run, not an estimate made months ago.
  • Predictable cash flow. Premium rises and falls with payroll.
  • No self-reporting. Payroll data flows to the carrier automatically. Premiums are calculated and paid each run.
  • Automatic payments through Relay. ACH premium transfers sync with each payroll cycle, eliminating manual payments and late remittance.
  • Class code accuracy. Codes applied per employee based on actual role and wages.
  • Confirmation every pay period. Email showing payroll reported, premium due, payment status, and reporting confirmation.
  • Competitive pricing. 160+ carriers compete for the business.
  • Fewer year-end surprises and better audit readiness. Consistent, organized records every period.
  • Time back. Less paperwork, more time running the business.

True Pay-As-You-Go: What It Is vs. What It Isn't

What it IS

  • Premium calculated on actual wages each payroll run through WOPR.
  • Payroll data sent to the carrier automatically, with no client involvement.
  • Premium collected by ACH in sync with each payroll through Relay.
  • Minimal upfront deposit.
  • Audits become a check of data already reported every period, not a year-end reconstruction.

What it ISN'T

  • It isn't a monthly installment plan. Splitting an estimated annual premium into 10–12 payments still leaves a true-up at audit.
  • It isn't self-reporting. If the client has to pull reports and submit them to the carrier, it's manual reporting on a different schedule.
  • It isn't a switch away from your payroll platform. The client stays with you.
  • It isn't audit elimination. Audits may still occur; they're cleaner and carry fewer surprises.
  • PayComp isn't the insurance carrier. PayComp supports placement, workflow, and reporting; coverage is issued by the carrier.
